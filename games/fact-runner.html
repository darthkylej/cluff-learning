How to play

  • A problem appears. Type the answer and press Enter.
  • Correct — a bright bling, the numbers flash green, and you take a step forward.
  • Wrong — a buzzer, the numbers flash red, and you slip a step back. The answer is shown, then the same problem comes back until you get it right.
  • The monster never stops, and it speeds up the longer you last. Distance is the only thing keeping it off you.
  • Negative answers are fine — type the minus sign, or tap ±.
  • Press Esc to pause.
