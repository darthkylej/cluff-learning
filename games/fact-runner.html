Practice ranges for your crew

Pick a runner, then choose which operations they practise and the numbers each one draws from. This is the whole of what they get asked — they have no say in it. Saved to their account, and live the next time they play.

 

How to play

  • A problem appears. Type the answer and press Enter.
  • Correct — a bright bling, the numbers flash green, and your pack fires you a step forward. A run of them lights the whole suit.
  • Wrong — a buzzer, the numbers flash red, and you stumble a step back. The answer is shown, then the same problem comes back until you get it right.
  • The monster never stops, and it speeds up the longer you last. Distance is the only thing keeping it off you.
  • Negative answers are fine — type the minus sign, or tap ±.
  • Press Esc to pause.
